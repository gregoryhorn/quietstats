Privacy, in plain view.

This ordinary page asks for no acceptance. Measurement is disabled by default. Local test reports belong to an isolated synthetic backend; a hosted instance requires explicit operator qualification and enablement.

Controller and contact

Not supplied. Each deployer must supply accurate controller, contact and applicable rights information before publication or live collection.

Minimal route reports

When explicitly enabled, only schema version, route-view type and an allowlisted route ID are sent. Receipt is totalled by server UTC day. The application stores encrypted aggregates, never individual analytics rows, raw IPs, referrers, browser history or visitor identifiers.

What is not collected

No analytics cookies, persistent browser analytics storage, fingerprinting, heartbeat, scroll, location, device, acquisition, outbound action or unique-person metrics. Owner access cookies are secure, HttpOnly and strictly same-site; they are separate from analytics. Recognized owner cookies exclude collection. An unrelated new browser has no owner identity.

Browser privacy signals

DNT, GPC, unknown signals, disabled policy and unqualified providers suppress reporting. Absence of an objection or continued browsing is not consent. No acceptance or preference record is created.

Retention, erasure and security

Daily aggregates are bounded to at most 400 UTC dates and may be pruned earlier for capacity. No raw history exists. Temporary security rate-limit digests are purpose-separated and expire within 24 hours; they are never joined to analytics. Session and recovery state are separately bounded. Small counts are suppressed, but repeated ranges may still permit inference.

Encrypted analytics backups are at most 2 MB each; retain no more than two for at most 35 days. Live erasure does not delete downloaded copies. Keep keys separately in existing protected custody. Data-key loss is distinct from authenticator loss.

Hosting and legal assessment

Provider delivery and security metadata, processor identity, location, transfers, applicable legal basis, objection requirements and rights processes remain unassessed. Aggregate or cookieless operation does not establish a blanket exemption. Production collection remains blocked until factual method and provider qualification. The local TLS certificate is self-signed and is not a production trust configuration.
